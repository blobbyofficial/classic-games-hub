Why it works

Snake feels fair because every mistake is legible. You can usually trace the exact turn that boxed you in.

How the run escalates

The opening is almost meditative. Then the trail length starts to steal your future options. Surviving longer means reading two or three turns ahead and keeping an exit lane open.

  • Early game Stay central and keep your movement smooth instead of hugging the walls too soon.
  • Mid game Resist sharp corner dives unless you already know the route back out.
  • Late game Treat empty space like fuel. A full board punishes panic more than slow planning.

Controls

Lean input set

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Pause with P. Restart with R. Mobile players also get on-screen movement controls in the playable page.
