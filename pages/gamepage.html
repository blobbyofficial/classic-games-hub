Snake Classic

Momentum game

Each fruit pickup adds another future mistake to avoid. The loop is immediate, readable, and ideal when you want a game that starts paying off in seconds.

  • Best for Players who like clean movement, memorized escape paths, and quick retries.
  • Control load Four movement directions, plus pause and restart.
  • Session shape Compact runs that spike in difficulty once the board gets crowded.

Tetris Deluxe

Recovery game

The board always asks for trade-offs: stack for perfect clears or spend a piece to survive. It rewards calm rhythm and fast correction when the matrix gets ugly.

  • Best for Players who like planning ahead, preserving space, and managing increasing speed.
  • Control load Move, rotate, soft drop, hard drop, pause, and restart.
  • Session shape Longer runs with difficulty pressure driven by level progression.
