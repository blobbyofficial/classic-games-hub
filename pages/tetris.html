Why it works

Tetris keeps asking for trade-offs. The tension comes from deciding when to chase a perfect setup and when to cash out for survival.

How the run escalates

The first few pieces let you establish rhythm. Then speed, height, and awkward shapes push every placement into a decision about future rescue paths.

  • Opening board Build a flat surface and keep one clean lane ready for long pieces.
  • Mid game Avoid stacking overhangs unless you are already managing the next correction.
  • Late game Burn pieces when needed. Survival clears are better than waiting too long for the perfect line.

Controls

Full keyboard loop

Move with the left and right arrows, rotate with the up arrow or X, soft drop with down, and hard drop with the space bar. Pause with P and restart with R.
